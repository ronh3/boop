## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Incoming command to active operation | Admission may reject, preserve, or supersede safety ownership. |
| Old generation to incoming generation | Timers and late server evidence must not release or mutate newer work. |
| Native queue replacement to automation | No attack, loot, target, or walk effect may enter between old and incoming owners. |
| Auto-flee to interrupt lane | Life-safety behavior must remain stronger than every queued operation. |
