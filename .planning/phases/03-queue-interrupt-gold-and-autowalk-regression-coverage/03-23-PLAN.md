<objective>
Give pickup and pack operations explicit ownership when a native queue replacement removes their already-sent command, so the command can be replayed exactly once after the interrupt releases and cannot later be abandoned by the replay's fresh timeout.

Purpose: Close the gold-side state-loss mechanism diagnosed in G-03-8 without extending timers, sleeping, clearing unrelated work, or treating elapsed time as terminal evidence. Put every pickup path on the operator-selected `full` queue while preserving packing on `freestand` and exact-owner/first-terminal semantics per D-01, D-02, D-05 through D-12A.
Output: A realistic native queue test model, pickup/pack displacement plus fresh-timeout/invalidation regressions, and generation-owned gold replay/evidence-wait state.
</objective>
